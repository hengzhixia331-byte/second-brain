## Welcome to MaizeGDB!

**MaizeGDB is a community-oriented, long-term, federally funded informatics service to researchers focused on the crop plant and model organism Zea mays.**  
  
If you use MaizeGDB in your research, please [cite us](https://maizegdb.org/cite).

21, April 2026: We are sad to share the news that [Major Goodman](https://maizegdb.org/person?id=12900) passed away on March 29, 2026. Major pioneered the classification of the global diversity of maize, the use of isozymes as genetic markers, and applied breeding to enhance the gene pool to US corn. Major started graduate school at North Carolina State University in 1960 and spent his entire career at NC State until his retirement in 2022, except for a three-year post-doctoral fellowship in Brazil. Major educated many graduate students, postdocs, and visiting scientists who have impacted crop improvement throughout the world. Major was elected to the National Academy of Sciences and received O. Max Gardner award from the University of North Carolina Board of Governors for "the greatest contribution to the welfare of the human race" in 1987. Major co-chaired the Global Maize Conservation Strategy committee, spearheaded the regeneration of global maize germplasm collections, and advised the public-private sector cooperative Germplasm Enhancement of Maize Project.
